Illuminated – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Summary

Illuminated does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

Your meditation sessions, reflections, notes, stage progress, and settings are saved locally on your iPhone. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No notification data leaves your phone.

Backups

Your data may be included in your device's iCloud or computer backups under your own Apple account settings. That is controlled by you and Apple, not by this app.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
